2026 Subaru Outback Wilderness Has 9.5-Inches of Ground Clearance & Adventure-Ready Gear

2026 Subaru Outback Wilderness Summary Points

  • The Wilderness trim is the most rugged and off-road capable version of the 2026 Outback.
  • Standard features include electronic dampers, chunky all-terrain tires, 9.5 inches of ground clearance, and a turbocharged Boxer engine.
  • The Subaru Outback Wilderness has inherited its predecessor's 3,500 lbs. towing capacity, but its updated SUV body offers more cargo space.

The 2026 Subaru Outback Wilderness has a potent yet familiar turbocharged 2.4-liter Boxer engine.
