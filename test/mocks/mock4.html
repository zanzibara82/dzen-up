2026 Subaru Outback Wilderness Has 9.5-Inches of Ground Clearance

2026 Subaru Outback Wilderness Summary Points

  • The Wilderness trim is the most rugged and off-road capable version of the 2026 Outback.
  • Standard features include electronic dampers, chunky all-terrain tires and 9.5 inches of ground clearance.

The 2026 Subaru Outback Wilderness has a potent yet familiar turbocharged 2.4-liter Boxer engine.

According to Subaru of America, the towing capacity is 3,500 lbs, see the official page for details.
